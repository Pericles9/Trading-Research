## 6. Conclusion

**The model achieves R² = 0.6235 on the out-of-sample test set**, indicating meaningful predictive power for forward Hawkes contagion. The key insight is not whether we can perfectly predict contagion, but whether
the model's **ranking** is useful — do events the model flags as high-contagion
systematically produce larger moves than those it flags as low-contagion?
