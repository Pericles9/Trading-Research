- **Integer-ratio diagnostic saturates at k = 20 and is uninformative above k = 16.** Phase 9 T1b's corporate-action diagnostic tests whether a flagged ratio sits within 3% of `k` or `1/k` for `k ∈ 2…20` (the prompt default, not tuned).
